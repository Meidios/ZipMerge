ZipMerge binary distribution notices
===================================

The complete current notice is docs/BINARY_DISTRIBUTION.md.

Public builds generate build/release-legal/ from hash-locked official sources.
The installer places that complete tree in _internal/legal/ and copies its
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt and the project MIT LICENSE to the installation root.

Run tools/build_release.py to prepare notices and build the installer.
Run tools/verify_bundle.py before distributing binaries.
